Payment Method Identifiers (PMIs) are used in web development to tell a website which payment options it can accept during checkout. They are included in code so browsers and payment systems can match the user’s available payment methods, like credit cards or digital wallets, with what the website supports. This helps make the payment process faster, more accurate, and more consistent across different platforms.
