Lab Reflection

The final project was very long but very fun and exciting to do. It was mainly every lab we did throughout the semester into one final project. For every part I added it reminded me of each lab I did. For the view menu button for example I just applied the same coding as lab 9 and changed details to fit my website. The table reminded me of lab 7 with the S tier table and so on. Overall, the lab was not hard but complex but I loved every bit of it. The challenging part that I did not continue was trying to get the user to select their pastry with quantity my version came out very poorly but I least I gave it try. I tested the site on three different devices and browsers, and it fit perfectly to each without being too small or too big. However, on the phone it did have some white space at the end and not on the computers.
This class taught me how to create a website and showed me what is really happening behind a website. Now when I enter a website I visualize the code behind it that brought it to life.
